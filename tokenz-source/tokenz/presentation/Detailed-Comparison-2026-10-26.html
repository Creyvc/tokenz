Text changed = 100 × (removed words + added words) ÷ (original words + shortened words). Words are compared with case and punctuation preserved. This measures wording, not meaning. Whitespace changes do not count as word changes.

Reduction = 100 × (original count − shortened count) ÷ original count. Negative reduction means growth. Local prompt tokens and provider request tokens can differ.

Quality retention = shortened score ÷ original score × 100. It can exceed 100%. A zero original score gives no defined ratio. High retention can occur when both answers are wrong. Exact checks test only their specified requirements. Not measured means unavailable, not zero.

Pipeline usage includes optimizer and shortened target calls, excluding the separate quality review. Shorter target inputs do not guarantee fewer pipeline tokens or financial savings.
