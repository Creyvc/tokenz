      AI models

      This option leaves your computer. Your prompt is sent to each model you tick below (Groq runs Qwen and GPT-OSS, Google runs Gemini), and each sends back a shorter version. NLP and TF-IDF never send anything.

      A rewrite that drops a number, a limit like “under 500”, a “not”, quoted text or a name like JSON, saves no tokens or comes back incomplete gets one retry with the reasons. If the retry fails too, the original is kept instead. Protected phrases aren't used here.
