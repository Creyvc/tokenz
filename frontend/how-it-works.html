How it works

Scroll

  1. 01

    Your prompt

    The prompt you'd send to an AI, pasted on Compare.

  2. 02

    Three methods

    They work on your words: NLP trims filler, TF-IDF drops off-topic sentences, AI rewrite asks Groq and Google.

  3. 03

    Shorter prompt

    Checked: numbers, limits and “not”s must survive, or the original is kept.

  4. 04

    Tokenizer

    Both versions cut into tokens and counted (o200k_base): the difference is what you save.

  5. 05

    Token IDs

    Each token is a number in the AI model's vocabulary.

  6. 06

    Embeddings

    In the AI model you send it to: each token becomes a vector, and you pay per token.
