How it works

  1. 01

    Paste a prompt

    On the Compare page, paste the prompt you would send to an AI.

  2. 02

    Choose a method

    NLP tidies the wording and TF-IDF cuts off-topic sentences, both on your computer. AI Rewrite sends your prompt to Groq and Google to be rewritten shorter.

  3. 03

    Compare the two

    See the original and the shorter version side by side, with the tokens each one uses. Check the shorter one still asks the same thing before you use it.
