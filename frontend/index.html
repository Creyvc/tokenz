Tokenz

Three Methods

Each one tries to get a complete answer from a single message. The question is whether the smarter ones save enough to pay for their own work.

01Runs on your computer

NLP

Tidies the wording: cuts polite openings and repeated sentences. Good for any prompt.

02Runs on your computer

TF-IDF

Cuts whole sentences that are off-topic. Best for longer prompts with background.

03Sends your prompt to Groq and Google

AI Rewrite

AI models each rewrite the whole prompt shorter. A rewrite that drops a number or saves no tokens is rejected, and the original is kept.

What We Measure

  1. 01

    Total tokens

    Every message, the resent history, and every helper call, until the question is fully answered.

  2. 02

    Messages

    How many times the person had to type before the answer was complete.

  3. 03

    Cost

    US dollars per 1,000 questions, using each provider's list prices from October 2026.

  4. 04

    Quality

    Scored 1 to 5 by raters who don't know which method wrote the answer.
