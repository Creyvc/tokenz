FAQ

What people ask before trimming a prompt.

Short answers about the three methods, where your prompt goes, and what we test.

What does Tokenz actually do?

It shortens a prompt before you send it to an AI, taking out the words that don't change what you're asking. The model reads fewer tokens, and the request stays the same.

Which of the three methods should I use?

NLP for any prompt: it tidies the wording, cutting polite openings and repeated sentences. TF-IDF for longer prompts with a lot of background, since it drops the sentences that are off-topic. AI Rewrite when you want the shortest version and don't mind your prompt being sent out to be rewritten.

Does my prompt leave my computer?

Not with NLP or TF-IDF: both run on your own computer. AI Rewrite sends your prompt to Groq and Google, so their models can write a shorter version.

Can a shorter prompt get a worse answer?

It can, so we check. AI Rewrite rejects any rewrite that drops a number or saves no tokens, and keeps your original instead. In our tests, every answer is scored 1 to 5 by raters who don't know which method wrote it.

Do the smarter methods really save money?

That's the question this project tests. AI Rewrite spends tokens of its own, so we count everything until the question is fully answered: every message, the resent history and every helper call, priced per 1,000 questions.
