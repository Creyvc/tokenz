Cookies

Tokenz asks before using any optional cookies, and you can change your choice at any time.

One essential cookie, tokenz_consent, remembers whether you accepted or denied. It lasts six months and stores nothing else.

Optional cookies, such as analytics to see how the site is used, only switch on after you click Accept All. If you click Deny All they stay off, and any set earlier are removed. Tokenz doesn't use any optional cookies yet, but anything added later will follow this rule.

The font is loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when the page loads. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
