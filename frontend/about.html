About

Tokenz is a group academic project, built in 2026 by a team of students.

The project

Prompts sent to large language models are often longer than they need to be, and every extra token adds cost and waiting time. Tokenz shortens a prompt before it is sent, keeping the parts the model needs to give the same answer.
